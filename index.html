How to add artefacts

Adding your artefacts

  1. Extract your artefacts in Clean Room and save the images in your approved Teams, OneDrive, or other authorized storage.
  2. In Teams, open the image and wait until it is fully loaded and clear. Select Copy Image, click an artefact space in the report, then press Ctrl + V (or ⌘ + V on Mac).

Remarks exceed the available space. Shorten them before exporting.
